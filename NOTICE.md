# Game content

McNeff Helper is a community-made companion for We Steal From Old People: The Game.

Game artwork, icons, character sprites, brick geometry, recipe definitions, and the bundled VT323 font originate from the supported Steam installation. Rights in that material belong to their respective owners.

The recipe data and brick geometry have been verified against Windows Steam version 0.1.4, build 26806474. Bundled artwork and the font were originally extracted from version 0.1.3; button skins and recipe icons were checked against the updated game.
